The Architectural Legacy

Elevating Foothill Grandeur to an Architectural Art Form

Tulsi Sahyadri by Raj Group represents a rare intersection of sculptural monolithic architecture and understated serenity. Nestled purposefully against the calm, green foothills of New Panvel and framed by the emerald contour of the Sahyadri mountains, each residence affords expansive panoramic outlooks and gentle hill breezes.

Crafted for connoisseurs of discerning taste, residences feature grand column-free volumes, floor-to-ceiling acoustic glass facades, high-speed elevator access, and cantilevered private sundecks that effortlessly blend refined indoor salons with boundless horizon skies.
