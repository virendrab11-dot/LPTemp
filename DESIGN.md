## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end, sovereign architectural aesthetic tailored for premier luxury real estate. Evoking permanence, sanctuary, and metropolitan distinction, the visual tone blends regal natural balance with polished executive refinement. 

Targeting discerning homebuyers, HNIs, and institutional property investors evaluating luxury residential offerings in New Panvel and greater Navi Mumbai, the interface commands trust, spatial prestige, and effortless exclusivity. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Classical Architectural Modernism**, characterized by:
- Deep, immersive royal forest and sapphire tones that provide foundational weight and privacy.
- Gilded metallic accents (warm champagne and burnished gold) applied with restraint for high-value highlights, badges, and focal dividers.
- Ample negative space mirroring grand entrance volumes, high-ceiling lobbies, and landscaped open horizons.
- Crisp hairline boundaries, muted natural stone layers, and tactile card surfaces that echo premier construction materials (granite, vitrified stone, aluminium anodised finishes).

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the royal Sahyadri mountainscapes and stately residential architecture:

- **Primary (`#0D2B22`)**: Deep Royal Emerald. Used for high-impact structural blocks, navigation headers, primary headline accents, and grounded hero framing.
- **Secondary (`#C29B38`)**: Warm Champagne Gold. Used exclusively for intentional prestige elements: MahaRERA verification badges, unit carpet callouts, focal CTAs, subtle border embellishments, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#1A384C`)**: Sapphire Slate. Bridges maritime connectivity (MTHL / Atal Setu proximity) with corporate credibility. Used for technical highlights, feature badges, and contrasting metadata cards.
- **Neutral (`#1E2421`)**: Deep Charcoal Spruce. Provides high-legibility body typography and crisp line-work while maintaining a subtle green undertone rather than sterile neutral black.
- **Background Tiers (`surface-ivory`, `surface-stone`, `surface-deep`)**: Clean architectural plaster and limestone hues that replace harsh clinical white, offering warmth and visual pedigree across light sections, alongside deep nocturnal emerald for immersive modal and gallery segments.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-fashion editorial gravitas with effortless programmatic legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical refinement and luxury architectural poise. Used across project taglines, section headers, tower identity points, and featured quotation areas.
- **Body & Subtitles (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp geometric clarity, ensuring technical property specifications (carpet area metrics, RERA compliance details, tower structural attributes) remain immediately legible.
- **Eyebrow & Metadata Accents (`label-caps`)**: Always rendered in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.14em`) and champagne gold or forest green tints to introduce sections with refined editorial polish.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a 12-column fixed-max grid (max container width: 1280px) to maintain balanced sightlines evocative of architectural plans and structural balance.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutter, generous 4rem outer canvas margin. Section vertical rhythms transition using `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` to preserve expansive breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.25rem gutter, 2rem margin. 3-column amenity matrices gracefully collapse into 2-column configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutter, 1.25rem canvas margin. Unit plan tabs and specification cards switch from multi-column comparative layouts to horizontal swipe carousels or cleanly stacked full-width modules.
- **Rhythmic White Space**: Dense real estate information is avoided. Specifications, location hubs (Hospitals, Connectivity, Schools), and unit codes are compartmentalized into structured cards with uniform internal padding of `space-lg` to `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal masonry and delicate light refraction rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Layered Architectural Tiers**: Surfaces step forward utilizing natural stone transitions. Base sections rest on `surface-ivory`, ascending to crisp white or deep `emerald-subtle` containers for cards.
- **Hairline Framing**: Primary cards and elevation dividers use crisp 1px borders rendered in `slate-line` (`#D8D3C8`) or translucent gold (`rgba(194, 155, 56, 0.25)`), evoking draftsperson precision.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: When cards elevate on hover, they produce an ultra-diffused, dual-layer shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(13, 43, 34, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 43, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Prestige Glow (Glass Overlay)**: On dark emerald panels, cards adopt a translucent backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur: 12px`) with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and subtle interior gold rim lighting.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** corner treatments. Sharp architectural geometry dominates the visual vocabulary, softened subtly to prevent harsh clinical friction:

- Default buttons, inputs, and specification tiles maintain a tailored `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, modal dialogue boxes, and imagery frames scale to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Badges (such as MahaRERA tags, Carpet Area tags, and "Under Construction" markers) utilize pill styling (`rounded-full`) to distinctly differentiate functional micro-metadata from structural layout geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Enquire / Schedule Private Site Tour)**: Solid deep emerald (`#0D2B22`) background with warm gold text or solid champagne gold (`#C29B38`) with deep emerald text. Padded at `14px 28px`, styled with `label-caps` or `label-md`, hairline hover shift, and a subtle warm gold glow on focus.
- **Secondary Action (Download Floor Plans / RERA Brochure)**: Ghost/outline button with a 1px border of `#C29B38` or `#0D2B22`, transparent background, displaying a smooth fill animation on hover.
- **Icon Buttons (Gallery Zoom / Floorplan Navigation)**: Square 44x44px architectural boundary, 1px border in `slate-line`, displaying gold icons.

### Badges & Chips
- **RERA Compliance Badge**: Deep emerald container with gold metallic 1px border, featuring a gold verified seal icon, crisp uppercase typography, and the registration number `P51700056125`.
- **Amenity & Status Chips**: Soft ivory surface (`#FAF8F5`) bordered by `#D8D3C8`, featuring monochromatic emerald icons and title case text.

### Unit Plan & Specification Cards
- **Configuration Cards (2 & 3 BHK)**: Crisp white card body surrounded by a 1px slate border. Header features the unit code (e.g., `Code 1230`, `Code 1640`) in gold badge styling, carpet breakdown (`681 sq ft + 93 sq ft`), and a high-contrast blueprint or floor plan view with clear zoom affordance.
- **Artist's Impression Disclaimer Bar**: Compact, understated caption rendered in `body-sm` with subtle italics along lower edge of all architectural renders.

### Inputs & Lead Forms
- **Fields**: Architectural minimal design with a bottom border or crisp 4-sided 1px box in `#D8D3C8`. Background remains pristine `#FAF8F5`. On active/focus state, border transitions cleanly to `#C29B38` with zero jarring glow.
- **Labels**: Persistent floating labels in `label-caps` rendered in deep forest charcoal.

### Location & Vicinity Matrix
- Structured list modules grouped by sector (Connectivity, Healthcare, Education, Retail). Each entry incorporates distance/infrastructure naming with subtle bullet rules and categorical icons.
